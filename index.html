MedGemma Blind Spots: Hematologic Oncology Evaluation

Systematic evaluation of Google's MedGemma-4B on hematologic oncology and CAR-T immunotherapy prompts — a clinical domain absent from the model's documented training data. Submitted as the Fatima Fellowship 2026 research challenge.

MedGemma-4B Clinical evaluation Hematologic oncology CAR-T immunotherapy Model safety

Tested both pretrained (medgemma-4b-pt) and instruction-tuned (medgemma-4b-it) variants across 13 probes: 2 in-domain controls in radiology and dermatology, and 11 hematologic oncology probes covering CAR-T mechanisms, trial data, differential diagnosis, treatment algorithms, and multilingual equity. Both models ran with greedy decoding for full reproducibility.

View dataset (HuggingFace)
Fatima Fellow 2026 · Selected from 700+
  • Confident fabrication of trial statistics: MedGemma-IT invents precise ZUMA-7 survival statistics. The actual published data (Westin et al., NEJM 2023) shows median OS not reached vs 31 months — the model generates different numbers entirely.
  • Persistent misdiagnosis: Both PT and IT models misidentify a textbook DLBCL presentation as follicular lymphoma, then recommend watchful waiting — clinically dangerous for untreated aggressive lymphoma.
  • Fabricated treatment regimens: IT model invents "R-IVAC" and "R-OBIN" — combinations that exist in no clinical guideline — as recommendations for relapsed disease.
  • Missed standard-of-care: For relapsed LBCL in a transplant-ineligible patient, model recommends R-CHOP (a first-line regimen) and omits CAR-T, glofitamab, or epcoritamab entirely.
  • Full 13-probe dataset with PT and IT outputs CC-BY-4.0. Ground truth citations: NEJM 2023/2025, Blood Advances 2025, MedGemma Technical Report (arXiv 2025).
